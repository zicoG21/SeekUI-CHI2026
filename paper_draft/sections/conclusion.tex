\section{Conclusion}

Target-absent GUI visual search exposes a forced-choice failure mode in target-present grounding systems. Post-hoc evidence accumulation with path and OCR signals improves not-found safety on an image-disjoint split and on an initial realistic validation set. Evidence-aware VLM verification provides a strong next-stage direction, but the primary result remains the interpretable combined path+OCR verifier until matched split validation is complete. The current results motivate a broader benchmark for uncertainty-aware GUI search with present, absent, semantic, and non-text targets.
