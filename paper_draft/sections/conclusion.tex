\section{Conclusion}

Target-absent GUI visual search exposes a forced-choice failure mode in target-present grounding systems. Post-hoc evidence accumulation with path and OCR signals improves not-found safety on an image-disjoint split and on an initial realistic validation set. Evidence-aware VLM verification further improves matched split performance, suggesting that screenshot reasoning and search evidence are complementary. The current results motivate a broader benchmark for uncertainty-aware GUI search with present, absent, semantic, and non-text targets.
