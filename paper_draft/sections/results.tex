\section{Results}

We separate the results into three layers. First, we evaluate the interpretable combined AND verifier with thresholds selected on development examples and tested on image-disjoint examples. Second, we evaluate the evidence-aware VLM verifier on the matched image split. Third, we compare generic and evidence-aware VLM judging on the full synthetic present/absent benchmark.

\subsection{Primary Interpretable Held-Out Result}

Table~\ref{tab:heldout_image_split} reports the cleaner held-out image split. Combined path+OCR verification improves absent F1 for both SeekUI and SeekUI-SFT, with bootstrap confidence intervals above zero. For SeekUI, absent F1 improves from 0.7347 to 0.8804 and accuracy improves from 0.7708 to 0.8692. For SeekUI-SFT, absent F1 improves from 0.6744 to 0.8279 and accuracy improves from 0.7325 to 0.8148. This is the main validation result because it avoids shared images across development and test examples.

\subsection{Evidence-Aware VLM Matched Split}

Table~\ref{tab:evidence_heldout_image_split} reports the matched image-split validation for evidence-aware VLM judging. On the same image-disjoint split, evidence-aware VLM improves SeekUI absent F1 from 0.7347 to 0.8962 and accuracy from 0.7708 to 0.8861. The bootstrap confidence intervals remain positive. This lets us frame evidence-aware VLM as the strongest validated verifier, while combined AND remains the main interpretable path+OCR method.

\subsection{Full-Benchmark Verifier Comparison}

Table~\ref{tab:main_results} summarizes the full synthetic benchmark and main baselines. Prompt-only SeekUI reaches 0.7300 absent F1, with 509 absent false-present errors. Cognitive stopping improves this to 0.8522, combined AND reaches 0.8824, and the evidence-aware VLM reaches 0.8981. Together with the matched split result, this shows that screenshot reasoning and scanpath/OCR evidence are complementary.

\subsection{Realistic Absent Validation}

Table~\ref{tab:realistic_absent_validation} reports the manually reviewed validation set. Combined AND best-F1 improves over prompt-only from 0.7907 to 0.9159 absent F1, reducing absent-to-present errors from 16 to 1. This is a small external-validity check, not a replacement for a larger real absent benchmark.

\subsection{Method Roles}

The methods play different roles rather than forming a simple leaderboard. Prompt-only SeekUI is the target-present baseline. Cognitive stopping isolates the contribution of scanpath evidence. Combined AND is the main interpretable method because its decision can be traced to path and OCR scores. Generic VLM yes/no prompting is a strong reviewer-risk baseline, but it is prompt-sensitive. Evidence-aware VLM is the strongest validated verifier because it combines screenshot reasoning with structured path/OCR evidence.

\subsection{Sanity and Filtered Sensitivity}

The synthetic absent benchmark is useful only if the absent labels are not trivially contradicted by known annotations or OCR artifacts. Our audit found few annotation conflicts, but a meaningful number of OCR-leak cases. We therefore ran filtered sensitivity analyses that exclude annotation-conflict and OCR-leak absent rows. The combined verifier remains stronger than prompt-only under this filtering, and the evidence-aware VLM retains a high filtered F1 on the kept examples. This supports the claim that the main effect is not solely an OCR leakage artifact, while still motivating a larger realistic absent benchmark.
